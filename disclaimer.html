ABOUT THIS SITE

Court forms are public. Understanding them shouldn't cost anything.

Docket & Deadline explains common California Judicial Council forms in plain English, for people navigating the court system without a lawyer.

What this is

Every guide on this site walks through a single official California court form: what it's for, who typically files it, realistic deadlines, and the mistakes that most often trip people up. The forms themselves are free public documents published by the Judicial Council of California — this site exists to make the instructions that come with them easier to actually read.

What this isn't

Docket & Deadline is not a law firm, not a lawyer referral service, and nothing on this site is legal advice. Reading a guide here does not create an attorney-client relationship. Court procedures, filing fees, income thresholds, and local rules change over time and vary by county — always confirm current requirements directly with your court's self-help center, the clerk's office, or courts.ca.gov before relying on anything here. If your matter is contested, time-sensitive, or involves real money or housing at stake, strongly consider consulting a licensed California attorney, including through a local legal aid organization if cost is a barrier.
